Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Introduction

This policy explains what information is and is not collected when you use TA SERIES. TA SERIES is a free, static website hosted on Cloudflare. It does not have accounts, login or registration. The site itself does not collect any personal information, and it sets no cookies, except for the feedback box (see Feedback below). It uses no analytics that identify visitors. TA SERIES uses GoatCounter for privacy-conscious website analytics. These statistics represent estimated visits and page views, not verified counts of unique individuals. No public visitor counter is displayed. Cloudflare, which hosts the site, receives your IP address when a page loads, to send the page back to you and to protect the site from abuse. GoatCounter also receives your IP address when a page loads, only to count the visit — it does not store it and does not use it to identify you.

Information we do not collect

The site itself does not collect any of the following:

  • Names, email addresses or passwords — there are no accounts, no login and no registration.
  • Cookies — this site does not set any cookies.
  • Advertising trackers — there are no ads and no advertising trackers on this site.
  • Analytics that track individual users — the site does not use analytics that follow or identify individual visitors.

GPA calculator

All GPA and CGPA calculations happen entirely in your browser, on your own phone or computer. The marks, name and institute you type are never sent to any server, and TA SERIES does not store them. If you download a result card as a PDF, that file is made on your device and stays with you.

Feedback

If you send a suggestion through the feedback box, it is saved on the TA SERIES website (Cloudflare) so the site owner can read it. Your name is optional. Your suggestion, and your name if you give one, are stored only to read your feedback. They are not shared with anyone else and are not used for ads.

Google Drive and GoFile files

Study material and results are hosted on Google Drive and GoFile and open in a new tab when you tap a file. Results open from GoFile first, with a Google Drive backup link. When you open a file, Google’s or GoFile’s privacy policy applies to that visit, not this policy. TA SERIES does not control how Google or GoFile handle information on their own services.

Email contact

If you email nurseofuture@gmail.com, your email address is used only to reply to you. It is not shared with anyone else and it is not sold.

Links to other sites

This site links to Google Drive and GoFile, where the study material and results are stored, and to WhatsApp for contact. Other sites have their own privacy policies, and you should read them if you want to know how they handle information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “Last updated” date above will change.

Contact

If you have a question about privacy, email nurseofuture@gmail.com.
