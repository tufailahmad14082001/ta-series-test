About TA SERIES

TA SERIES is an independent educational resource platform created to help BS Nursing students find academic material more easily. The platform brings study resources, examination results and revision publications together in one organised place.

What TA SERIES is

TA SERIES is an independent educational resource platform for BS Nursing students. It brings study material, examination results and revision books together in one place, organised so students can find what they need in a few taps. Everything on the site is free, and every file opens from Google Drive.

Educational purpose

TA SERIES exists to help BS Nursing students find relevant academic resources quickly — the right slides, book or past paper for their semester and subject, without searching through scattered files and groups.

Who it serves

The platform serves BS Nursing students across all eight semesters, from Semester 1 to Semester 8.

Types of resources

The site holds three collections — :

  • Study Resources ( files) — lecture slides, books, MCQs, syllabus, study notes and past papers for semesters 1 to 8, including the 36-file TA SERIES Slides Book.
  • Examination Results ( files) — BS Nursing exam results by region, semester, batch and examination session.
  • TA SERIES Publications ( books) — Past Papers: revision books and solved past-paper collections, including a Biostatistics Definitive Edition.

How resources are organised

Study material is organised by semester, subject and resource type. Examination results are organised by region (Hazara, Kohat, Malakand, Mardan, Peshawar), semester, batch (20-24 to 25-29) and examination session. Regular results (Normal, Fall, Spring) are shown separately from special results (Summer, Winter, Makeup).

Mission

To make nursing study material easier to find and accessible to BS Nursing students.

The Story Behind TA SERIES

Tufail Ahmad founded TA SERIES after experiencing firsthand the challenges of finding quality study materials during his BS Nursing journey. What started with a single collection of lecture slides gradually grew into a free academic library of covering all eight semesters of BS Nursing.

Built through dedication and a student-first vision, TA SERIES aims to make nursing education more accessible by ensuring students can find the resources they need, when they need them.

Our mission is simple: Making Nursing Simple. Making Students Strong.

Editorial information

Resources on TA SERIES are collected and organised by the founder. Files are linked from Google Drive and open in a new tab. No institutional approval is claimed for any resource. If a link is broken or a file is wrong, students can report it from the Contact page.

Ownership and independence

TA SERIES is independent. It is not affiliated with, approved by or endorsed by any university, college or examination board. Students should cross-check examination results and academic information with the relevant official institution.

Educational disclaimer

TA SERIES is an independent educational resource platform designed to support BS Nursing students. Academic information and examination documents should be cross-checked with the relevant official institution. The availability of a resource on this website does not necessarily imply institutional endorsement.
