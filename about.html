About TA SERIES

TA SERIES is a free study library for BS Nursing students. You can find slides, solved past papers, MCQ practice tests, examination results and a GPA calculator in one place.

Who made it

TA SERIES was started by Tufail Ahmad, a BS Nursing student at GINAHS, Dera Ismail Khan. He found it hard to get good study material, so he began with one collection of lecture slides. Today the library covers all eight semesters.

Independence and accuracy

TA SERIES is independent. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any university or examination board. Please check results and academic information with the official institution.

How files are collected

Resources are collected and organised by the founder. Files open from Google Drive. If a link is broken or a file is wrong, tell us on the Contact page.
