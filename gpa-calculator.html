Grading Scale

This is the KMU BSN grading scale, verified from one KMU BSN result sheet — Final Result of B.Sc. Nursing Generic, 8th Semester Spring 2024, dated 28.01.2025.

MarksGradePoints
90–100%A+4.0
80–89%A4.0
75–79%B+3.5
70–74%B3.0
65–69%C+2.5
60–64%C2.0
Below 60%F0.0

There is no D grade — below 60% is F. GPA = sum of (grade points × credit hours) ÷ sum of credit hours, rounded to 2 decimal places.

Please note: this calculator uses the KMU grading scale verified from one KMU BSN result sheet (8th Semester Spring 2024, dated 28.01.2025). Your official GPA is the one on your KMU result/transcript.
