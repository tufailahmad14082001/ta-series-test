What TA SERIES is

TA SERIES is a free, non-commercial educational platform for BS Nursing students. It makes no money from the files on this site. It does not sell any book, paper or result, and it does not charge students to use the site.

What is on this site

The content on this site comes from different sources:

  • Made by TA SERIES: past-paper books, chapterwise MCQs, quizzes and study books written by TA SERIES itself.
  • Published by universities: examination results, which universities publish and share publicly.
  • Shared by teachers and students: lecture slides and study notes, shared for study. Much of this material is already widely shared in public student groups.
  • Publicly available material: past papers and study resources that circulate freely among students.

TA SERIES does not claim to own any file that belongs to someone else. Study materials belong to their original authors and publishers.

Lecture slides

Lecture slides on this site were shared by teachers and students for study. They are used only for education, not for sale. If you are a teacher, college or institution and you do not want your slides on this site, you can ask for them to be removed. The same takedown process below applies to slides, notes and any other file.

How to ask for a file to be removed

If you own a file on this site, or you represent the owner, and you want it removed, email nurseofuture@gmail.com. Please include:

  • The name of the file.
  • The link to the page or file on this site where you found it.
  • Your name and how you are connected to the file (author, publisher, teacher or institution).
  • Any proof that you own it, if you have it (for example, the book cover, the publisher page or your name on the file).

We will remove the file from the website and from its public link, normally within 48 hours of receiving your email. We will reply to your email to confirm it has been removed.

What we do when a file is removed

When a file is removed, its name and link are taken off the website completely, so students can no longer find or open it from TA SERIES. The file itself is kept privately and is not shared again. If the same file is reported again, it will not be put back.

For students

Please use the resources on this site for study only. Do not sell TA SERIES material, and do not redistribute it commercially. If you know that a file on this site belongs to someone who would not want it shared, please tell us through the Contact page.

Contact

For any copyright question or takedown request, email nurseofuture@gmail.com. You can also reach TA SERIES on WhatsApp at +92 305 5959410.
